# Media Plan

- Hero: real glass-front salon photo with transform-only scroll depth and a static fallback.
- Studio carousel: four distinct official Google Maps photos; automatic one-step advance every 3 seconds, manual arrows, thumbnails, and lightbox.
- Product detail: real professional product photo from the listing.
- Final CTA: real storefront photo to strengthen recognition and trust.
- Performance: all photos resized, metadata stripped, and encoded as WebP; no heavy canvas animation or scroll-scrubbed video.
- Accessibility: meaningful alt text, reduced-motion fallback, keyboard-compatible buttons, and high-contrast actions.
